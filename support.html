What should I include in a support request?

Tell us which app you use, its version, your device model and Android version, and the steps that led to the issue. A screenshot can help; hide personal information before sharing it.

AR Drawing: my camera won’t open.

Allow camera access for the app in Android Settings, close other apps using the camera, then reopen AR Drawing. If it still fails, send us your device details.

AR Drawing: how do I trace from the screen?

Place thin paper over the lit screen. Adjust brightness and opacity, then lock the template to keep it still while you trace gently.

I need help with a purchase.

Contact us with the app name and Google Play order ID if available. Never send card details or passwords. Manage subscription renewal through Google Play.

I want to ask about my data.

Visit the Privacy center, select your app, or email us with “Privacy request” in the subject. Include the app name and the kind of request.

Can I download Draw Music?

Draw Music is an idea we’re exploring. It is not available to download.
