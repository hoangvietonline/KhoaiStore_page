TWO WAYS TO FIND YOUR FLOW

Your paper.
Your preferred setup.

Choose how you’d like to trace.
Both modes keep the drawing in your hands.

01 / CAMERA

Draw with your camera.

Keep your phone steady above your paper. Look at the reference over your camera view and follow its lines on the page below.

  • Adjust template opacity to see your reference and paper together.
  • Move or zoom the reference to fit your drawing.
  • Lock the image position when you’re ready to trace.
Setup tip: use a stable phone stand and keep your paper well lit.
02 / SCREEN

Trace from your screen.

Place thin paper over your phone and trace the visible lines. A simple setup for a quiet moment of drawing practice.

  • Adjust screen brightness and template opacity.
  • Move and zoom the image before you begin.
  • Lock the template so it stays in place as you trace.
Setup tip: trace gently, and use paper thin enough to see the reference.

A FEW THINGS YOU MIGHT WONDER

Before your
first sketch.

More help
Do I need to know how to draw?

You can start with a simple reference or a guided lesson. Follow the lines slowly and repeat a step as often as you like.

Does the app draw on the paper for me?

You make the drawing yourself. The app provides a reference through your camera view or phone screen to help guide your pencil.

What do I need for camera drawing?

Use a pencil, paper, and a stable phone stand. Keep the phone steady above the paper and make sure your drawing area is well lit. Camera access is required for this mode.

What kind of paper works for screen tracing?

Thin or tracing paper makes the screen reference easier to see. Adjust screen brightness and template opacity, and press gently while tracing.

Do I need internet access?

Lesson and template content may need to download before use. Google Play purchases and other online services also need a connection.

Where can I get help or find privacy information?

Visit our Support page for help with setup or purchases. The Privacy center links to the policy for AR Drawing Sketchar.
