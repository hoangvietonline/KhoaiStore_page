Find your way to draw

Put a sheet of paper over your phone and trace the visible template from the screen, or use your camera to line up a drawing reference with your paper. Adjust the image to suit your setup and make each sketch your own.

Choose a template, set up your paper, and follow along at a comfortable pace.

Learn one small step at a time

Follow friendly lessons that break a drawing into manageable steps. Practice lines, notice new shapes, and celebrate progress as your picture comes together.
